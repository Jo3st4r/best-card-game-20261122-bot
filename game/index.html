Goal: reduce the Stormwright to 0 HP before it does the same to you. Both start at 30 HP.
Your turn: click a card to play it (it costs mana, top-left gem). Mana refills each turn and grows by 1, up to 8. Draw 2 cards a turn. Click End Turn when done.
The tides: Ember deals damage, Kelp shields and heals, Tide draws cards, steals mana, and hits harder the fuller your hand is.
Surge: play two cards of the same tide in one turn and the second one gets +2 power.
Read the Stormwright: the middle of the table telegraphs what it is about to do — shield up, heal, or race it.
